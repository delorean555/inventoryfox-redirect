Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a workspace?
Tap "My Workspaces" on the home screen or press the + button from the Inventory tab. You can add a name, icon, color and cover photo.
How do I scan a barcode?
Tap the Scan button on the home screen or item form, then point the camera at a barcode or QR code. The item will be found instantly or you can create a new one with the code pre-filled.
How do I export my inventory?
Go to the Reports tab and tap PDF Report, or use the Export Data option in Settings to export as CSV or Excel. Pro and Max plans support all export formats.
How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings > Export, Import, Backup and tap "Back Up Now". Your full inventory will be saved as a .json file. Available on Pro and Max plans.
How do I restore a purchase?
Go to Profile > Subscription and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.
My data is not syncing. What should I do?
Check your internet connection and make sure you are signed in. Pull down to refresh on the home screen. If the issue persists, sign out and sign back in.
