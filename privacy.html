Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2025

Inventory Fox ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by OniksTech. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Inventory Fox mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you register or sign in with Google.
  • User Content: Inventory data you create, including workspaces, items, stock movements, photos, and documents you upload.
  • Identifiers: A unique user ID assigned to your account, and a device ID used for subscription management.
  • Purchase History: Information about your in-app subscription (Pro or Max plan) processed through Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Crash Data: Diagnostic data such as crash logs to help us identify and fix technical issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (inventory management, cloud sync, backup).
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • To process and manage your subscription.
  • To send notifications you have opted in to (low stock alerts, expiry date reminders).
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Backend database and authentication provider. Stores your account information and inventory data. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and in-app purchase management. Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Optional authentication method. Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase cloud servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data, spaces, items, and uploaded files will be permanently deleted. Purchase history may be retained as required by applicable laws.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Export your inventory data at any time using the in-app export feature.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@onikstech.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Inventory Fox is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
